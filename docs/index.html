Features

Everything to stay on top of your subscriptions

Built with Flutter for iOS and Android. Backed by Supabase for real-time sync and your own database.

📋
Full subscription stack

Track every service in one place — name, price, billing cycle, start date, next renewal. Multi-currency support built in.

🔔
Renewal reminders

Get notified today, 3 days, or 7 days before each charge. Configurable per your preference. No surprise renewals.

💰
Savings tracking

Every time you cancel or pause a subscription, SubscriptTrack records the monthly and annual savings so you can see the impact.

📊
Spending analytics

Total monthly spend, breakdowns by currency, and a list of renewals coming up in the next 30 days — always in view.

🔒
Private by default

Your data lives in your own Supabase project with Row Level Security. Nobody can see your subscriptions — not even us.

📡
Works offline

Add and update subscriptions without a connection. Changes sync automatically when you're back online.
